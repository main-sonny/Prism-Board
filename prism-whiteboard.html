Shift-click to select multiple objects. Drag empty space to box-select. Drag the bottom-right selection handle to resize. Double-click text or a note to edit. Hold Shift for squares, circles, or 45° lines. Drop or paste images to add them. Pinch with two fingers to zoom. The eraser removes whole objects. Everything works offline.
